## The word

**Micromanager** — the boss, client or manager who signs off on your work
and checks every keystroke, every comma, every pixel while doing it. A wry
observation, not a plain label: funny at once, true of exactly the human
the bounty asks about, vivid and specific instead of flat, and it lands on
its own with no explanation needed.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, deterministic, no network)
writes exactly that one word to `/output/entry.txt` — 12 characters,
no trailing newline.
